Canvas rhythm and nested composition

Share a ruler. Rebase deliberately.

A page canvas gives unrelated regions one 64-bit horizontal measure. A nested canvas starts a new measure for local composition, trading page-level alignment for modularity inside a narrower container.

Recommended default

One page canvas, one rhythm

Consecutive 20- and 24-track projections inherit the same ruler. Their internal lines form related polyrhythms; their outer measure remains shared.

The lattice stays real in every rhythm
