Semantic composition

Keep the wrapper. Keep the lines.

gp_subgrid lets meaningful nested content inherit the tracks occupied by its parent. Unlike a nested canvas, it continues the existing measure instead of starting a new one.

Why subgrid

Three structures, one editorial problem

Flattening exposes every piece to the page grid but removes the story wrapper. A normal wrapper restores semantics but creates its own approximate columns. Subgrid preserves both hierarchy and the parent’s exact track lines.

Usage

Continue geometry; do not rebase it

Apply the utility to a direct child of a Graph Paper grid. The wrapper still needs a parent placement, and its descendants choose lines within that inherited span.

Use subgrid when nested semantic content should continue the outer geometry. Use gp_canvas instead when a component needs independent coordinates and responsive behavior.
