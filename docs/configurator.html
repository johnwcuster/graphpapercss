Ruled-field policy

  
The ruled field runs from bit 4 to bit 60, with two-bit gutters. Explicit end markers belong to internal gutters and sit on whole-bit lines. Stacked compositions suppress them. Thin fields do not automatically center a rule in their one-bit gutters.
