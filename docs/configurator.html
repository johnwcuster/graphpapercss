Gutters

Carries forward until another gutter choice. Changing the layout keeps your gutter choice.

Ruled uses a 56-bit field. Field edges and full-width items have no rule. For column layouts, the export includes ruler measurement and end-rule ownership automatically. Mosaics use Neat or Thin.
